USAGE EXAMPLES
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Enable Pod Lead in Exports:
  1. Click Settings (⚙️)
  2. Check "🏆 Pod Lead in Exports"
  3. Click Save
  4. Export data → CSV will have PodLead column

Enable Pod Lead in Copy Logs:
  1. Click Settings (⚙️)
  2. Check "🏆 Pod Lead in Logs"
  3. Click Save
  4. Copy any log → Entries will show "[Lead: Name]"

Copy Queue + Names:
  1. Click "📋 Queue+Names" button in Queue Distribution section
  2. Paste to see analysts grouped by queue

Remove Analyst:
  1. Click analyst name to view details
  2. Click "🗑️ Remove" button
  3. Confirm to remove from tracking
